Changed branch/field
--------------------
The changed eligibility branch is qualityProbeEligible in
backend/internal/application/service/account_quality_monitor.go. When
source_group_id is configured, the probe set now includes accounts bound to
source_group_id, accounts bound to the configured degraded_group_id, and
accounts carrying a valid older account_quality_routing_group_id marker.

The changed recovery branch is restoreQualityGroups in the same file. Existing
account_quality_original_group_ids remains authoritative. If that marker is
absent, a healthy account is rebound to source_group_id only when its complete
current binding is exactly [degraded_group_id] and recovery_threshold has been
reached. Additional or changed manual group bindings are preserved.
